**Turn your network into outcomes.**

DeepVista brings Relational Intelligence to the calls, emails, and connections you already have. It turns them into a living map of your network so you can find the right people, see who can introduce you, and decide how to reach out.

## How DeepVista works

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Find the people who actually fit.**">
    DeepVista explains why each person fits what you're building and drafts a message tailored to them. Each match you rate helps improve future recommendations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**See exactly who can introduce you.**">
    Explore the warm paths in your network to the person you want to meet. DeepVista ranks them by tie strength so you can see who's best placed to make the introduction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**A personal connection plan for every person.**">
    DeepVista maps your options across warm introductions, LinkedIn, and email. It recommends the best route and tailors each message to the person you're reaching out to. Once you approve the plan, DeepVista runs it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core concepts

<Card title="Goal">
  A workspace for one outcome. It keeps your List, Trust Graph, and Context Cards together.

  *“I’m raising a seed round for my healthcare AI startup.”*
</Card>

<Card title="List">
  A table for finding and researching people in batches. Chat with the agent to research a person, add a column, or update a cell.

  *“Find 20 seed investors who back healthcare AI and add a column for typical check size.”*
</Card>

<Card title="Trust Graph">
  A map of everyone on your List and how they’re connected to you. See how someone could help you reach your goal, understand what makes a warm intro credible, and track relationships over time.

  *“This investor looks promising. Who could introduce me, and how do they know each other?”*
</Card>

<Card title="Context Card">
  A record for a person, company, email thread, or research note. Open a person card to review past conversations and research before you reach out.

  *“What did we talk about last time, and what should I follow up on?”*
</Card>
