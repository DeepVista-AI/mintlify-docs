# Welcome to DeepVista

DeepVista captures how you work and ensures your team and agents follow your intent — without you repeating yourself.

DeepVista turns your GTM busywork into reusable workflows for marketing, sales, and customer success. It closes one continuous growth loop that acquires, engages, and retains your customers.

## Core concepts

**Context Card**

Context cards capture **entities.**

Every team member, customer, meeting note, file, and session is stored within a context card.

**VistaBase**

VistaBase understands **relationships.**

Every time you chat with DeepVista, VistaBase automatically updates its understanding of your GTM busywork into one connected knowledge base.

**Workflow**

With an accurate understanding, agents **act** on real-time intelligence through a set of defined workflows.

**WorkBench**

WorkBench gives you and your team control in a project.
Search context cards, check scheduled jobs, and share knowledge across your team.

## How DeepVista works

**Dump your thoughts here anytime.**

DeepVista turns your unstructured, raw input into context cards.

**Watch agents run your playbook automatically.**

DeepVista creates workflows personalized to how you work, then executes them end-to-end.

**Collaborate with your team.**

DeepVista works best when everyone in a project shares knowledge with one another.
