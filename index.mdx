## What is DeepVista?

DeepVista turns your GTM busywork into reusable workflows for marketing, sales, and customer success. It closes one continuous growth loop that acquires, engages, and retains your customers.

## Core concepts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Context Card" icon="id-card">
    Context cards capture **entities**. Every team member, customer, meeting note, file, and session is stored within a context card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="VistaBase" icon="database">
    VistaBase understands **relationships**. Every time you chat with DeepVista, VistaBase automatically updates its understanding of your GTM busywork into one connected knowledge base.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Workflow" icon="diagram-project">
    With an accurate understanding, agents **act** on real-time intelligence through a set of defined workflows.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="WorkBench" icon="table-columns">
    WorkBench gives you and your team control in a project. Search context cards, check scheduled jobs, and share knowledge across your team.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How DeepVista works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dump your thoughts here anytime">
    DeepVista turns your unstructured, raw input into context cards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch agents run your playbook automatically">
    DeepVista creates workflows personalized to how you work, then executes them end-to-end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collaborate with your team">
    DeepVista works best when everyone in a project shares knowledge with one another.
  </Step>
</Steps>
