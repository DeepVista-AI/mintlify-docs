## What is DeepVista?

DeepVista turns your GTM into automated workflows for marketing, sales, and customer success. You'll get one continuous loop of automated playbooks that acquire, engage, and retain, extract insights. 

## Core concepts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="VistaBase™" icon="database">
    **VistaBase™** is the shared ontology of your business. It structures everything your project captures — sessions, notes, files, people, organizations, topics, and more — into one connected knowledge model, so your team and agents always have the full context behind every workflow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="WorkBench" icon="id-card">
    WorkBench is the space where each stage of your customer flywheel happens, Acquire, Engage, Retain, and Insights, one connected loop feed directly into the next round of acquisition, keeping the workbench current with the latest customer context. 
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Automate GTM busywork">
    Save successful customer operations as reusable workflows you can launch, customize, and improve across your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live execution">
    Watch AI agents execute tasks in real time. Every action is transparent, observable, and can be interrupted or edited at any moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto-capture customer context">
    Every execution enriches your customer knowledge. People, conversations, workflows, and outcomes connect into a living graph.
  </Step>
</Steps>
