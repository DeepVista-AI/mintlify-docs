## What is DeepVista?

DeepVista turns your GTM busywork into reusable workflows for marketing, sales, and customer success. You'll get one continuous loop of automated playbooks that acquire, engage, retain, and enrich your customer knowledge.

## Core concepts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="VistaBase" icon="database">
    **VistaBase **is the shared ontology that structures everything your project captures — sessions, notes, files, people, organizations, topics, and more — into one connected knowledge model, so your team and agents always have the full context behind every workflow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="WorkBench" icon="id-card">
    **WorkBench** is your home surface for finding a person, note, workflow, or knowledge entry - scoped to whatever part of the business you're focused on right now.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Chat with DeepVista.">
    Just as you what you naturally would do with a chatbot. Ask a question. Drop some thoughts.  DeepVista will turn unstructured, raw input into structured ontology.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute your workflow. ">
    Watch DeepVista turns the conversation into a structured workflow that personalized to how you work and execute end-to-end tasks. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with your team. ">
    Launch, customize, and improve proven workflows across your team. 
  </Step>
</Steps>
