DeepVista builds the Social World Model. We turn fragmented calls, emails, and networks into a living relationship graph, helping teams uncover warm paths and close high-value deals.

# Core concepts

<Card title="Context Card">
  Context card captures **entities.**

  Every person, company, note, tracker, and email draft lives in a card. Person cards log your Gmail and LinkedIn message history, pull recent social activity, and hold fit scores and AI-enriched properties.
</Card>

<Card title="VistaBase">
  VistaBase maps **relationships and warm paths.**

  As you chat, drop notes, or run research, VistaBase links everything into a live knowledge graph centered on your profile. 
</Card>

<Card title="Workflow">
  Workflows **execute** on your context.

  Workflows read the notes and fit criteria in your workbench to source leads, enrich tracker columns on demand, and draft or send personalized outreach across Gmail and LinkedIn on a schedule.
</Card>

<Card title="WorkBench">
  Workbenches give each goal **its own scoped space.**

  Type a goal to spin up a dedicated workspace for one ICP, campaign, or product line. Each workbench keeps your Notes, Workflows, and Trackers in one place your team can share.
</Card>

# How DeepVista works

<Steps>
  <Step title="**Batch-research your target accounts and leads.**">
    Define your target profile in chat, import a list, or drop raw notes. DeepVista structures prospects into a live tracker table, researching custom attributes and buying signals on demand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Map warm paths through the relationship graph.**">
    Switch to Graph View to trace connections between you, your team, and your target contacts. DeepVista maps investments, past colleagues, alumni networks, and mutual connections to replace cold outbound with warm intros.
  </Step>
  <Step title="**Run personalized outreach and capture what works.**">
    Draft messages that account for past message history and recent prospect activity. Every reply, objection, and closed deal feeds back into your project memory to sharpen the next campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>
