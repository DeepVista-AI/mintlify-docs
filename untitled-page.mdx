title: "Research Lead and Outreach "
---

automate lead research and outreach with DeepVista. 

Step 1: input your company website/ your industry. DeepVista will grab the context. 

Step 2: Tell DeepVista the goal of this research. A few examples you can try:

Here's our pitch deck and website. Learn our positioning and voice so you can draft outreach as me.

Find protocols with meaningful TVL that don't currently offer any insurance or cover layer to their users.

Rank this list of protocols by urgency, weight recent incidents higher than TVL size.

Draft a first-touch message to \[target\]'s founder referencing \[their recent incident/audit/TVL milestone\] and pitching our differentiation.

run, show the data in vistabase.
