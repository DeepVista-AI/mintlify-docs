## Who should read this

This guide is designed for **founders, marketing operators, and developer advocates** who want to maintain an active social presence and respond instantly to inbound interest.

You should follow this playbook if you:

- **Receive inbound messages or comments on LinkedIn and X** and want contextually relevant replies drafted automatically.
- **Want to capture leads from social interactions** directly into your VistaBase CRM tracker.
- **Convert internal notes, product updates, or trending topics** into ready-to-publish X posts.

## 1. LinkedIn Auto-Reply

Auto-reply to new LinkedIn messages triggered by a webhook using a Unipile account.

- Please sign up an DeepVista account at [https://app.deepvista.ai](https://app.deepvista.ai)
- Please sign up an Unipile account at: [https://dashboard.unipile.com/signup/](https://dashboard.unipile.com/signup/)
