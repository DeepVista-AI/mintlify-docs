title: "Version 0.7.2"
description: "New ways to guide the AI, organize contacts across your databases, and onboard new projects, plus a smoother chat-to-asset UI."
---

August 31, 2026

#### **1. Leave instant feedback on chat responses**

Rate and leave notes directly under any message the agent generates.

If an answer misses the mark or needs adjustment, submit a quick score and comment right in the thread. This feedback helps us spot patterns and tune agent behavior faster.

![Rating a chat response with a score and optional comment](/images/release-0-7-2-chat-feedback.gif)

#### **2. Aggregate people across workbench databases**

Workbenches now roll up people across all your databases into one master roster, instead of making you click through separate lead lists or search outputs.

Every outreach search or contact import you run adds to that same unified view.

![Browsing people and records aggregated across a workbench's databases](/images/release-0-7-2-aggregate-people.gif)

#### **3. Redesigned project onboarding flow**

Starting a new project is now faster with a guided setup.

When you create a project, the flow walks you through setting up your initial workbenches, workflows, and goals so you can start working right away.

![Picking a starting point when setting up a new project](/images/release-0-7-2-project-onboarding.gif)

#### **4. Inspect generated cards without losing your chat flow**

When the AI creates a note, database, or document mid-conversation, it now opens automatically in the right side panel.

You can inspect new outputs right away without clicking around or losing your place in the chat.

![A generated card opening automatically in the side panel during a chat](/images/release-0-7-2-side-panel-cards.gif)
