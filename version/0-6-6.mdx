July 20, 2026

Welcome to this week's DeepVista release update. This week we shipped smarter workspace organization, fairer team credit sharing, and dynamic AI model routing.

---

## What we shipped this week

### Smarter workspace context and filters

- **Filter by attributes:** Quickly narrow down notes, workflows, or context cards in VistaBase using targeted metadata attributes.

- **Artifacts and type correction:** We've reserved **Notes** exclusively for human-written context and created a dedicated **Artifacts** card type for AI-generated memories and code snippets. If an AI card is ever misclassified, you can manually correct its type in the card options.

### Fairer credit allocation

- **Project-level credit usage:** Workflow executions now deduct credits directly from the **project owner's balance**, rather than charging individual team members who trigger the run.

### Intelligent model routing and generation metrics

- **Automatic model selection:** DeepVista now dynamically routes your prompts to the optimal model. Simple chat queries hit lightweight, fast models like **GLM** or **Kimi**, while heavy reasoning and task-oriented jobs route to models like **Claude**.
