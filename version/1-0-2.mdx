September 30, 2026

#### **1. Create a workbench for each initiative**

A workbench gives each initiative one home for its context, workflows, and results. Start from a template like **Lead Research & Outreach**, or choose **Skip for now** for a blank canvas.

![Creating a new workbench from a template or a blank canvas](/images/release-1-0-2-create-workbench.gif)

Try it by clicking **Create New Workbench** on your workbench page and picking a template or **Skip for now**.

#### **2. Drop raw context into Notes**

Paste in discovery takeaways, interview quotes, objections, and rough thoughts as they are. Your agent searches Notes on its own to find real decision-makers and draft emails that quote actual customer problems.

![Pasting raw call takeaways into the Notes tab of a workbench](/images/release-1-0-2-notes-context.gif)

Try it by pasting call takeaways or user feedback into the **Notes** tab of your workbench.

#### **3. Run or create a workflow to repeat verified work**

Refine your lead criteria and email drafts with your agent first. Once you find the angle that gets replies, turn it into a workflow and repeat it.

![Running a workflow and adding a new one in a workbench](/images/release-1-0-2-run-workflow.gif)

Try it by running a workflow in your workbench, or click **Add workflow** to automate steps that already work.

#### **4. Track results in the Database**

The Database records verified leads, email statuses, and booked meetings as your agent finishes tasks, so you can check results in one place.

![Opening the Database tab to review leads, outreach stages, and booked calls](/images/release-1-0-2-database-results.gif)

Try it by opening the **Database** tab to inspect leads, review outreach stages, and track booked calls.
