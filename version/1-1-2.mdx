October 17, 2026

Know who you're looking at before you commit: candidates that arrive as real profiles, the legal terms in the app instead of off it, and one idea running from the homepage to your sign-in screen.

#### **1. Review candidates as real profiles, not names on a list**

- The wizard's **Which of these look like your customer?** step now runs a single search agent instead of a hand-built query pipeline, and each candidate comes back as a profile with their photo, role, company, and LinkedIn link.
- Profile links always come from the search results, never from the model, so every candidate opens a page that actually exists.
- Every person you score becomes a row in the new workbench's tracker — photo, role, company, and LinkedIn, alongside the score and the reasons you gave — so the people table isn't empty on day one.

Try it by starting a new workbench and reviewing the faces on **Which of these look like your customer?**

#### **2. Read the Terms and Privacy Policy without leaving DeepVista**

![Terms of Service hosted in the app, with an On this page index](/images/release-1-1-2-terms-of-service.png)

- **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** are now pages in the app at `/terms-of-service` and `/privacy-policy`, instead of links that sent you off to another domain.
- Each one opens with an **On this page** index that tracks the section you're reading as you scroll.
- The links under the sign-in form, in **Settings**, and in the site footer all point here now — and both pages open without signing in.

Try it by clicking **Terms of Service** under the sign-in form.

#### **3. One idea, from the homepage to your sign-in screen**

![The Social World Model on the sign-in screen](/images/release-1-1-2-social-world-model.png)

- The sign-in screen and the site footer now carry the same sentence: DeepVista builds the **Social World Model**, turning fragmented calls, emails, and networks into a living relationship graph.
- The homepage leads with the **THE SOCIAL WORLD MODEL · RELATIONAL INTELLIGENCE** marker, closes with *Powered by the Social World Model*, and its browser tab now reads **DeepVista | The Social World Model**.
- The sign-in screen carries that same marker and the same headline you land on from the homepage, so the handoff no longer changes the subject.

Try it by opening the sign-in screen and reading the line above the form.
