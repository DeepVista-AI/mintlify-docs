description: "DeepVista is now a unified outbound engine, taking prospects from lead research to sent outreach with a redesigned workbench and closed-loop automation."

September 19, 2026

#### **1. Work from one unified workbench**

Prospecting, campaigns, and research now live in a single three-tab workbench: Tracker, Workflows, and Notes. Manage prospects, run campaigns, and document research without switching between separate tools.

Try it by opening any workbench and moving between the Tracker, Workflows, and Notes tabs.

#### **2. Let closed-loop automation run your pipeline**

New inquiries and prospect lists now trigger qualification, message generation, and tracker syncing automatically. Records update on their own as prospects respond or move through sales stages, so your tracker stays current without manual updates.

Try it by adding a new prospect list or inquiry and watching the tracker update as it moves through the pipeline.

#### **3. Get set up with guided onboarding**

New workspaces now follow a four-step setup: define your target customer profile, build your initial prospect database, configure outreach campaigns, and activate dispatch.

Try it by creating a new workspace and following the onboarding flow from profile to dispatch.
