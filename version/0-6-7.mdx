July 27, 2026

This week we unlock 4 light workflows so you can run automated playbooks without keeping your laptop open or losing track of background work.

**Highlights**

- **Run 24/7 background workflows without keeping your laptop open.** DeepVista now hosts and executes background tasks on cloud machines with 1-click browser terminal access.
- **Upload files and talk to any content type in chat, with password protection.** Upload documents, audio, and attachments directly into chat, with a 1-click private mode to pass API keys and passwords safely.
- **Never miss a completed task with a unified notification inbox.** Replaced scattered, hard-to-follow workspace alerts with one central notification feed showing what needs your attention.
- **Connect AI agents directly to product docs and LinkedIn.** Connect live Mintlify docs and LinkedIn accounts directly to your project so agents can look up setup guides and manage outreach automatically.
