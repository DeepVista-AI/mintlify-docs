September 22, 2026

#### **1. Get verified LinkedIn profiles for new leads**

Lead discovery workflows now focus on verified contact profiles. When DeepVista finds prospects for your pipeline, it fills in verified names, roles, and clickable LinkedIn URLs, leaving unconfirmed fields blank instead of guessing.

![Running a Lead Discovery workflow to see verified LinkedIn profiles fill in for new leads](/images/release-0-7-9-verified-linkedin-leads.gif)

Try it by running a Lead Discovery workflow or checking your leads table.

#### **2. Set workflow schedules with one click**

Change how often your workflows run without digging through extra menus. Click the schedule link next to any workflow pause button to pick a preset frequency, such as "Daily at 9:00 AM," or enter a custom schedule.

![Clicking the schedule link next to a workflow toggle to set a run frequency](/images/release-0-7-9-workflow-schedule-one-click.gif)

Try it by clicking the schedule link next to any workflow toggle in your workbench.

#### **3. Start chats with full context from suggestions**

Clicking an action button on your homepage or workbench suggestions now opens a chat with your context already loaded. DeepVista automatically pulls in the relevant cards and task details, so you can review recommendations and act without retyping anything.

![Clicking an action button under Things to do to open a chat with full context loaded](/images/release-0-7-9-chat-full-context-suggestions.gif)

Try it by clicking any action button under Things to do on your workbench overview.
