October 13, 2026

Hey there,

DeepVista now helps you find precise leads.

Last week, we shipped quite a few improvements, built from testing with our users (thank you!) and our own internal dogfooding.

Here’s our learning: Don’t hand over your judgment on who is the right person to an agent. You have the richest context there is, from customer discovery calls, from your read on the industry you’re in.

The problem is, most of that is tacit; you’ve never had to say it out loud.

We improve the system so that the agent learns alongside you, challenges the assumptions you got wrong, and holds itself to the reality test: who replies, who closes, logged as it goes.

Here’s the flow.

#### **Judge 5 people, and surface the signals you care about.**

![Judge 5 people, and surface the signals you care about](/images/release-1-1-0-judge-5-people.png)

Most tools filter on superficial signals like team size and funding stage.

What actually moves the needle is in deep understanding about the person: for example, how active the founder is on LinkedIn, whether the team already has the role that would use your product, whether they just posted a job for that role. We change the onboarding flow by letting you score the first 5 candidates, and the agent starts looking for exactly those signals on your next search.

Looks past headcount and funding stage, at founder activity, team composition, and recent job postings.

Your reasoning becomes the agent’s criteria.

In our tests, one round of scoring made the next batch 40% more accurate.

How: In the new project, “Find me [who you’re looking for].” Score the 5 people it shows you, fit or not, and say why.

#### **Reach out in batch. Goodbye generic templates.**

![Reach out in batch](/images/release-1-1-0-batch-outreach.png)

90% of cold outreach gets ignored, usually wrong channel, wrong time, or a message that shows you don’t actually know who they are.

A LinkedIn connect request first, with a short intro before any pitch, is a warmer path in than a cold email. What someone posts tells you what they already have opinions about can become your real wedge.

Connect first, short intro, before any outreach message.

Their posts tell you the topic to open with, not a guess.

Ask the agent why one person replied and another didn’t, and use that to challenge your own assumptions about who’s actually a fit.

How: In chat, “Draft outreach for these 5 leads.” Treat your agent as a thinking partner: “Why did this one reply and that one didn’t?”

#### **One database. Remember every customer and what to do next.**

![One database for every customer](/images/release-1-1-0-one-database.png)

Once you’ve reached out to enough people, don’t hold it all in your head. The agent will remember it for you, and your whole team can share the relevant context: who you talked to, what they said, what’s next.

Every touchpoint, call note, and meeting note lands in one place, visible to the whole team.

Hand a deal to whoever’s booking the call or closing it, and they already have the context.

Ask the agent who to follow up with, or who to tell about a new update, and it already knows.

How: In chat, “I have a new update, who should I tell?” or “Log this call, what’s the next step for this person?”

Above is how you can find precise leads on DeepVista.
