- **Clean data separation, no ghost rows.** Meeting notes, call transcripts, and campaign playbooks stay linked in the background instead of creating blank rows in your list.

- **Full version history.** Every row added, removed, or updated shows up in one unified timeline.
- **Smart import and a cleaner grid.** Paste multi-cell data from Excel or Google Sheets without broken formatting. Column borders and draggable resize handles make dense trackers easier to scan.

## What we shipped this week

### Clean data separation (no more ghost rows)

Trackers now separate actual data records from linked reference context.

- **Dedicated row architecture:** attaching background research notes or a campaign playbook to a tracker no longer creates a blank "ghost" row. Trackers isolate real data items from reference links.
- **Connected background context:** call notes, customer emails, ICP briefs, and campaign workflows stay attached to your tracker in the background, fully accessible to the AI without cluttering your data grid.
- **Interactive card rows:** every row is still a full context card. Click any contact or campaign post to open its profile, past communications, and associated tasks.

  ![Attaching background research to a tracker row no longer creates a ghost row](/images/release-0-7-0-clean-data-separation.gif)

### Non-destructive AI views

- **Prompt-driven filtering and sorting:** tell DeepVista "show only high-fit inbound leads from this week's social campaign" or "sort CRM contacts by outreach date and hide internal notes."
- **Non-destructive view layer:** view changes touch only display rules — filters, sort order, visible columns. Your context cards and campaign records stay untouched.
- **Persistent saved layouts:** custom views save automatically, so you and your team return to the same column setup every time.

### Versioned rows and a unified history

- **Versioned row additions and removals:** trackers record the exact revision when a row is added, completed, or removed.
- **Unified changelog:** row edits and tracker setting changes merge into one chronological activity history.

- **Draggable column resize handles:** adjust column widths to fit long company names, LinkedIn URLs, and status tags.

## Try these on DeepVista

#### 1. Build an intelligent CRM tracker

Prompt DeepVista with your target customer profile:

```text
Research 10 target companies matching our ICP and create a CRM tracker with columns for Company, Contact, LinkedIn URL, and Outreach Status.
```

DeepVista builds the tracker and populates verified records. Paste in call notes or screenshots and ask DeepVista to update the matching row.

#### 2. Launch a 10-day social media campaign

Give DeepVista a tutorial, landing page, or release note and prompt:

```text
Research this content and propose 10 social copies targeting our ICPs with lead capture forms linked to our CRM tracker.
```

DeepVista schedules the campaign in a tracker, generates unique tracking links, and routes inbound form submissions straight into your CRM.

#### 3. Filter your pipeline with plain English

Open your CRM tracker and prompt:

```text
Filter this tracker to show only leads who received an outreach email, and sort them by the last touchpoint date.
```

DeepVista updates your saved view immediately and leaves the original records untouched.
