title: "Version 0.6.9"

August 11, 2026

This week we expand DeepVista's connectivity engine — connecting live third-party apps, running real-time deep research with Exa Agent, and introducing interactive chat decision controls during conversations.

**Highlights**

- **Connect external software natively with Composio MCP & OAuth2.** Securely pass managed credentials and grant agents live access to third-party APIs without manual key configuration.
- **Batch-find lead emails with Exa Agent.** Run live web queries directly inside agent workflows for up-to-date market intelligence and company research.
- **Guide your agent to act more precisely in chat.** Make choices via structured checkbox widgets (`<ask>` blocks) instead of typing out manual instructions.
- **Organize projects easily with agent-created Workbenches.** Let agents automatically create custom Workbenches and pin your favorites for instant access.
- **Trace card lineage and attribution.** See which chat session or workflow execution generated every context card in your project, for complete auditability across team and multi-agent runs.

Remote collaboration and context organization across projects are also getting smarter — [again](/version/0-6-8). You can now co-edit a doc with multiple team members in real time, and ask the agent to drop your recent output (a note, a workflow, or any context card) into a Workbench so it's easy to find later.

## Try these on DeepVista

### 1. Enable Composio and Exa deep research

Connect live APIs and query real-time web content natively inside your agent workflows. To enable Composio, go to **User → Composio**. To enable Exa Agent, go to **Experimental → Enable Deep Research**.

<Frame>
  ![Enable Composio and Exa deep research](/images/release-0-6-9-exa-deep-research.gif)
</Frame>

### 2. Answer `<ask>` blocks inline

Prevent your agent from guessing missing context or stopping mid-workflow to ask open-ended questions. When an agent presents options during a run, click your preferred choices directly on the interactive card to resume execution.

<Frame>
  ![Answer ask blocks inline](/images/release-0-6-9-ask-blocks.gif)
</Frame>

### 3. Organize outputs into a Workbench

Keep growing projects structured automatically, without manual folder creation and navigation. Ask your agent to organize outputs into a new Workbench.

<Frame>
  ![Organize outputs into a Workbench](/images/release-0-6-9-workbench-organization.gif)
</Frame>

### 4. Trace a card back to its source

Open any context card's details panel to view the chat sessions and workflow executions that produced it.

<Frame>
  ![Trace a card back to its source](/images/release-0-6-9-card-lineage.gif)
</Frame>
