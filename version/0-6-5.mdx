### Seamless collaboration with context card locking

When multiple teammates work together, keeping edits safe matters.

- **Edit locking:** If you open a card someone else is editing, a banner tells you the card is locked.
- **Take over:** If you need to edit immediately, choose **Take Over**. The other person's banner updates right away. This works across workflows, nodes, and context cards.

<Frame>
  ![Context card edit locking](/images/ex5-3.gif)
</Frame>

- **Advanced filtering:** Stack multiple filters on tables to find specific users or records in seconds.

### Turn your local machine into a DeepVista agent

You can now execute tasks on your local hardware or remote cloud servers with the DeepVista CLI.

- **Local task delegation:** Run a background command on your machine (Mac, Windows, or Linux) so it polls DeepVista's task queue.
- **Run complex workflows remotely:** Prompt DeepVista from your phone, and it can dispatch scripts or processes — like posting to social media — to your active machine at home.
