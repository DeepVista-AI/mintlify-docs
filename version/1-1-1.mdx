October 15, 2026

Judgment that sticks: your whole history with someone on their card, a fit call you can trust anywhere you see it, and your tools where you'd expect to find them.

#### **1. Walk into every conversation knowing your message history**

![Message History on a person card](/images/release-1-1-1-message-history.png)

- Every touch you've had with someone now logs to their **person card**, so you don't have to dig through Gmail before you reply.
- Click **Write message** and the agent finds the relevant touchpoint, such as something you said you'd follow up on, and drafts from it.

Try it by opening a person's card and checking **Message History** before you write to them.

#### **2. Know which workbench needs you the moment you land**

![Workbenches need you on Home](/images/release-1-1-1-workbenches-need-you.png)

- If you run outreach across several accounts or clients, the morning question is "which one needs me today," not "let me open each one and check."
- Home now splits into **Workbenches need you** and **Everything else**, so the answer surfaces by workbench instead of sitting in one long list.

Try it by opening **Home** and checking **Workbenches need you** first.

#### **3. Calibrate your list. Your agent learns who to look for next.**

- Mark anyone **Great fit**, **Maybe**, or **Not a fit** from their card or the onboarding wizard, while you're already looking at them.
- Hover the score to see the reason, like "actively hiring for GTM roles, no recent fundraising news," instead of a bare number.

Try it by opening any **person card** and setting their fit right there.

#### **4. Find your connected tools in the main nav**

![Connectors in the main nav](/images/release-1-1-1-connectors.png)

- **Connectors** now lives in the main nav, not buried in Settings.
- Connect LinkedIn and you land back on **Connectors** with a confirmation that it worked.

Try it by opening **Connectors** from the main nav and checking what's connected.
