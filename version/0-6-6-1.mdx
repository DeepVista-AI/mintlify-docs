---
title: "Version 0.6.6.1"
---

July 25, 2026

This release makes the editor a lot more forgiving, adds audio to your notes, and gives project owners real control over credits and infrastructure. Here's what shipped.

---

### **🎧 Audio Notes & Inline Playback**

- **Upload audio directly:** Drop `.mp3`, `.wav`, or `.m4a` files straight into the markdown editor and play them back inline — no downloads, no external players.
- **Automatic file cards:** Every audio upload generates its own `file` context card, so recordings live in your knowledge base alongside everything else.

---

### **✍️ A Much Sturdier Editor**

We spent this cycle removing the sharp edges from long editing sessions.

- **No more lost work on lock expiry:** If your edit lock lease expired while you were still typing, the editor could go blank and drop changes. Both the data loss and the blank screen are fixed.
- **Reload grace window:** Refreshing a tab no longer lets a secondary tab snatch the edit lock out from under you.
- **Tab key stays put:** Pressing Tab inside a task list or ordered list no longer blurs the editor by jumping to checkbox and chevron controls.

---

### **🗂️ Better Organization & Navigation**

- **Move notes to folders:** A "Move to folder" action is now available directly in the card action menu, and the menu itself is reordered so the actions you use most are at the top.
- **Inline workflow step editing:** Workflow step popovers now include an **Edit** button that focuses the editor immediately, so you can tweak a step without leaving your place.
- **Drag the chat bubble anywhere:** The floating chat trigger can be dragged to any of the four screen corners, and it remembers where you put it across sessions.
- **Cleaner shared cards:** "Shared by" badges no longer appear on cards you own yourself, and view-only tabs once again update live while someone else edits.

---

### **💳 Credits & Plan Upgrades**

- **Upgrade in place:** Project owners now see **Compare all plans** and **Upgrade** controls right in the project credits view.
- **Low-balance alerts:** A zero credit balance is flagged in red so you find out before a run fails instead of after.

---

### **⚙️ Under the Hood**

- **Project-level VM provisioning:** Standalone VM management moved from the user level to the project level, with an admin VM interface backed by Terraform-managed Google Cloud compute.
- **More proactive local agents:** Delegated tasks now auto-retry up to three times, and long-running workflows use non-blocking deferred confirmation instead of stalling on a prompt.
- **Linear issue sync (internal):** An internal Linear issues tab syncs `issue` cards into your knowledge base carrying their Linear attributes — identifier, assignee, priority, and status.
