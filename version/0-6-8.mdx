August 6, 2026

Collaborating with team members and agents is getting smarter on DeepVista.

**Highlights**

- **Organize your project with Workbenches.** You can now group workflows, context cards, and scheduled jobs, and find them easily on the homepage.
- **Transfer your understanding of relationships between entities to your agents.** In your prompt, mention a person card, a note on a customer call, and a workflow to create connections for your agent.
- **Research smarter by chatting.** You can now create an intelligent table card that can populate any information you want just by chatting with DeepVista.
- **Turn your thinking process into executable workflows with one click.** Drop in a screenshot or any unstructured thought, and watch DeepVista convert it into a workflow ready to execute.
