## Overview

By the end, every form signup will trigger an automatic Discord alert, with DeepVista handling the two-way webhook wiring so you don't write any code.

Connecting your front-end web form to a Discord notification uses a simple two-way webhook process:

1. **Inbound:** Send form submission data from your website manager (Framer, Webflow, and similar) into DeepVista.
2. **Outbound:** Route that parsed data from DeepVista into your Discord channel.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

1. A DeepVista account with an active project and a workflow ready to receive form data
2. Access to your website manager's form settings (for example Framer or Webflow)
3. Permission to create webhooks in the Discord server where you want alerts

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link DeepVista to your website manager">
    In your DeepVista workflow, generate an inbound webhook URL and copy it. In your website manager (such as Framer), paste that URL into your signup form's webhook settings. DeepVista can then receive each form submission automatically.

    ![Setting up website form webhook](/images/web1.gif)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Train the workflow with a UI screenshot">
    Take a clean screenshot of your signup form. On the DeepVista canvas, upload the screenshot with a prompt like:

    > My input form looks like this, please update the workflow.

    DeepVista analyzes the layout and maps the form fields to your workflow logic.

    ![Training DeepVista with UI screenshot](/images/web6.gif)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate a Discord webhook">
    In Discord:

    1. Open **Server Settings** → **Integrations** → **Webhooks**
    2. Click **New Webhook**, name it, and choose your notification channel
    3. Click **Copy Webhook URL** and save it

    ![Creating a Discord Webhook](/images/web11.gif)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable live channel notifications">
    Return to your DeepVista workflow canvas and paste the Discord webhook URL into the outbound notification node. DeepVista posts a success message to that channel on every new signup.

    ![Finalizing DeepVista to Discord link](/images/web4.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

***

<Tip>
  **What happens next?**
  Submit a test signup on your live form. You should see a new workflow run in DeepVista and a matching alert in your Discord channel.
</Tip>
