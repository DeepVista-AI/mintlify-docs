## Overview

For lean technical teams, running high-touch outbound sales creates an operational dilemma: either founders spend hours on manual lead research, or they hire expensive SDR headcount prematurely.

This tutorial demonstrates how to build a generalized, human-agent lead research and outreach engine in DeepVista across three core workflows:

1. **Market Research & Account Profiling**
2. **Outbound Lead Generation & Enrichment**
3. **Inbound Qualification → Proposal Generation**

<Frame>
```

                   │

```
</Frame>

<Info>
  This workflow is aimed at founders, CTOs, and GTM leaders running lean remote technical startups (5 to 15 employees), but the pattern generalizes to any high-touch, low-volume outbound motion. It only requires a connected Gmail account, so it runs entirely as a Light Workflow. No local machine or MCP server setup needed.
</Info>

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

1. A DeepVista account with an active project
2. [Gmail connected](/tutorials/gmail-daily-summary-workflow) to DeepVista, so drafted outreach can be reviewed and sent
3. A target account list, or a process for inbound inquiries to land in DeepVista

<Note>
  You don't need to build or edit anything in VistaBase™ yourself. Everything below happens through chat: you describe the account and ask DeepVista for what you need, and DeepVista structures it into VistaBase behind the scenes.
</Note>

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell DeepVista about the account">
    In chat, paste in the inbound inquiry or describe the account you want to research: company name, domain, and who the decision maker is.

    ```text
    Add this account for research: [Company Name] (target-domain.com), a
    [industry] company with a 5-person remote team. Decision maker is
    [Name], [Role], [email].
    ```

    DeepVista turns this into a company profile and a contact profile in VistaBase™ and links them together automatically. You don't need to create or manage these yourself.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask DeepVista to research and score the account">
    Ask DeepVista to look into the account and check it against your ideal customer profile (ICP):

    ```text
    Research this account and score it against our ICP.
    ```

    DeepVista checks public records, the company's domain, and other public signals, then scores the account:

    | Criteria | What DeepVista checks | Weight | Example Score |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Team Size | Lean, fully remote (5 to 15 employees) | High | 10/10 |
    | Use Case Fit | High-value technical sales, limited GTM bandwidth | High | 10/10 |
    | Technical Readiness | API-driven, cloud-native, or smart-contract based | Medium | 8/10 |
    | Purchase Intent | Active inquiry, explicit pricing inquiry, or funding event | High | 9/10 |

    You'll get an overall score (e.g. 9/10 = Strong Match) and a recommendation on whether to fast-track outreach. DeepVista adds these findings as notes on the account, so anything you'd already written about it stays untouched.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask DeepVista to set up a client workspace">
    If the account is in a regulated industry (insurance, financial services, healthcare) or otherwise needs its data kept separate, ask DeepVista to isolate it:

    ```text
    Set up a separate project for [Company Name]. They'll need [GDPR
    compliance / a specific data residency requirement].
    ```

    DeepVista creates a dedicated project so this client's data and research stay walled off from every other client's, and configures it to meet the compliance need you named, including full deletion on request if GDPR applies.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask DeepVista to draft the outreach email">
    Once the account looks like a good fit, ask DeepVista to write the first outreach message:

    ```text
    Draft an outreach email to [Name] at [Company Name] using what we
    found in the research.
    ```

    DeepVista writes a draft personalized with the research from Step 2, referencing the company by name and what it found about their setup:

    ```text
    Hi [Founder Name],

    Saw your recent work scaling [Company Name]'s technical infrastructure
    with a lean remote team.

    For technical teams balancing product development with sales execution,
    DeepVista provides human-agent workflows to automate market research,
    account profiling, and inbound lead qualification within isolated
    project workspaces.

    We support strict data residency and complete GDPR compliance out of
    the box.

    Would you be open to reviewing a live workflow configured for
    [Company Name]'s outbound lead qualification this week?

    Best,
    [Your Name / Team]
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the draft and ask for a follow-up reminder">
    Open the draft in your workbench, edit anything you'd like to change, and approve it to send. DeepVista never sends outreach without this step.

    Then ask DeepVista to keep track of the response:

    ```text
    Remind me to follow up if I haven't heard back in 3 business days.
    ```

    DeepVista schedules a check-in for you: it looks for a reply, and drafts a follow-up if you haven't gotten one.
  </Step>
</Steps>
