## Create a Unipile Account

To create an account, [sign up here](https://dashboard.unipile.com/signup/).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select LinkedIn as your API">
    When selecting APIs, check **LinkedIn**.

    <Frame>
      ![Unipile signup form with LinkedIn selected under APIs](/images/linkedin-auto-reply-select-api.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your LinkedIn account">
    <Frame>
      ![Connecting a LinkedIn account from the Unipile dashboard](/images/linkedin-auto-reply-connect-account.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your webhook URL from the workflow card">
    <Frame>
      ![Copying the webhook URL from the DeepVista workflow card](/images/linkedin-auto-reply-webhook-url-card.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a webhook and paste the URL">
    <Frame>
      ![Creating a Unipile webhook with the copied callback URL](/images/linkedin-auto-reply-create-webhook.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate an access token and copy the command">
    <Frame>
      ![Generating a Unipile access token](/images/linkedin-auto-reply-access-token.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your DSN">
    You'll find it in the top-left corner of the sidebar.

    <Frame>
      ![Unipile dashboard with the DSN highlighted in the sidebar](/images/linkedin-auto-reply-copy-dsn.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide the command and DSN to DeepVista">
    ```text
    Here's the command and the DSN: <paste the copied command and DSN from Unipile>
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the setup">
    Upon success, you should see confirmation of the setup.

    <Frame>
      ![DeepVista confirming the Unipile connection and workflow setup](/images/linkedin-auto-reply-confirmation.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're ready to go">
    You're ready to auto-reply to any new LinkedIn messages.
  </Step>
</Steps>
