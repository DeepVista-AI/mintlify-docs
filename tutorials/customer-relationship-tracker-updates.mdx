## Capture a Note or a Screenshot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take a note or capture a screenshot">
    Take notes the way you normally would, then open that note's chat to work with it directly.

    <Frame>
      ![A discovery-call note with its Chat action highlighted](/images/tracker-note-example.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask DeepVista to update the tracker">
    Copy and paste it in chat, ask DeepVista:

    ```text
    Add customer A to my CRM Tracker.
    ```

    DeepVista will automatically update the row.

    <Frame>
      ![The note linked to a person card and the DeepVista Customer CRM Tracker card](/images/tracker-linked-cards.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Monitor Your Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask DeepVista to monitor your inbox">
    Ask DeepVista:

    ```text
    Monitor my email inbox and update the CRM Tracker every day.
    ```

    DeepVista will create a **scheduled job** to run the monitor **workflow** automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get alerted on updates">
    Get an email alert when a CRM contact has an update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check recent interactions">
    On the CRM tracker, click to check the **Person Card** for recent interaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>
