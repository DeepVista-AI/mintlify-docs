title: "4. Keep Your Relationship Tracker Auto-Updated"
description: "Maintain clean pipeline visibility and eliminate manual CRM entry after every call or reply."
---

## Log Touchpoints & Call Notes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update the tracker from a conversation">
    After a call or customer chat, paste the notes or transcript in and ask DeepVista to apply them:

    ```text
    Here are my notes from today's call with [Company Name]. Update
    their tracker row: [paste notes or transcript]
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auto-Advance Pipeline Stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Auto-advance the pipeline stage">
    DeepVista reads the conversation for commitments, sentiment, and action items, then moves `stage` forward if it should (e.g. Lead to Pilot), sets a dated `next_action`, and adds the summary as a comment on the account's card, without touching your original notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Query Context Before Next Action

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull full context before you reach out">
    Before your next call or reply, ask DeepVista for the account's full context (current stage, last touch, prior notes), pulled straight from the tracker you've been keeping updated, so nothing gets re-explained or missed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
