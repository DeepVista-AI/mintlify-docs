## Capture a Note/ a Screenshot

1. Copy and paste it in chat, ask DeepVista to "add customer A to my CRM Tracker."

   DeepVista will automatically update the row.

## Monitor your Email

1. Ask DeepVista: Monitor my email inbox and update the CRM Tracker everyday.

   DeepVista will create a **Scheduled job** to run the monitor **workflow** automatically.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-inbox-monitor-cards.png)
</Frame>

2. Get an email alert when a CRM contact has an update.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-inbox-alert-email.png)
</Frame>

3. On the CRM tracker, click to check **Person Card** for recent interaction.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-person-card-interaction.png)
</Frame>
