## What you'll get

- **10 days of ready-to-post content**, delivered to your inbox each day.
- **A unique tracking link for every post**, so you can see which post drives leads.

## Who should read this

- **Founders & Marketers**: Turn existing content into a steady drumbeat of social posts without writing from scratch.
- **Growth Leads**: Know exactly which post is driving inbound leads.

## What you'll learn

- Build a content library from a piece of content you already have (a landing page, release note, or tutorial).
- Turn that library into a 10-day campaign with a lead capture form on every post.
- Get your daily post emailed to you and start tracking which posts convert.
- Get notified the moment a lead comes in, and follow up automatically.

## 1. Research & Create a Content Library

### Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have a DeepVista account with an active project.

To create an account, [sign up here](https://app.deepvista.ai/login?redirectUrl=%2Fsign%2520in).

### Ask DeepVista to research and draft your content library

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide an input DeepVista can research">
    Provide an input — your landing page, a product release note, a tutorial, or any content asset you already have.

    For example, we used this prompt:

    ```text wrap
    Research published tutorials from https://docs.deepvista.ai/ and propose 10 copies targeting my ICPs.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the generated post copy">
    DeepVista researches the source content and drafts 10 posts targeting your ICPs.

    <Frame>
      ![DeepVista generates a post from your source content](/images/social-media-campaign-show-post.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Build a 10-Day Campaign

### Schedule the campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title='Ask DeepVista: "Create a 10-day campaign on this."'>
    DeepVista schedules your content library into a 10-day campaign, one post per day.

    <Frame>
      ![DeepVista content library scheduled across a 10-day campaign](/images/social-media-campaign-content-table.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add a lead capture form to each post

<Steps>
  <Step title='Add a lead capture form to each post'>
    Use this prompt:

    ```text wrap
    Let's add an input form in each post. When someone fills it out, update the CRM tracker.
    ```

    DeepVista creates a Lead Capture Workflow for the campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your form link">
    Hover over the upper-right corner of the workflow card and generate a URL for your input form.

    <Frame>
      ![DeepVista's lead capture workflow](/images/social-media-campaign-lead-capture-workflow.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the form">
    On the workflow page, click **Input** > **Input Form** > **Add input** to get a sample prompt you can edit.

    <Frame>
      ![Publishing an input form from the workflow](/images/social-media-campaign-customize-form.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your tracking link">
    Each post also gets its own unique tracking link — you'll find it on the workflow card.

    <Frame>
      ![Finding the unique tracking link on a post's workflow card](/images/social-media-campaign-tracking-link.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
