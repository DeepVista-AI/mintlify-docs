## Overview

Finding high-fit leads and crafting hyper-personalized outreach usually requires juggling multiple tabs, manual web searches, and fragmented notes.

With the **Lead Research & Enrichment** workflow, DeepVista automates this end-to-end pipeline:

1. Sources candidates based on your target ICP segment.

2. Researches company fundamentals, TVL/AUM, funding stage, and key decision-makers.

3. Scores and prioritizes leads against your exact criteria.

4. Persists enriched `organization` and `person` cards into VistaBase, automatically linking them in the knowledge graph.

5. Stages tailored outreach drafts `type=email`) for your final review before sending.

This tutorial walks through invoking the workflow, configuring inputs, and reviewing the generated context cards.

## Why DeepVista for Lead Research?

- **Persistent Context**: Unlike standalone scraping tools, DeepVista checks your existing VistaBase cards first to deduplicate contacts and build on prior session notes.

- **Staged & Safe Execution**: Outreach drafts are persisted as unsent email cards `type=email`). Nothing is sent externally without explicit review and approval.

- **Graph Knowledge Base**: Every enriched lead, key contact, and outreach draft is cross-linked into your knowledge graph, ensuring full traceability across your pipeline.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

1. A DeepVista account with an active project.

2. The `Lead Research & Enrichment` workflow installed in your workspace (or available in the Marketplace catalog).

3. (Optional) An active Gmail connector configured in **Settings → Data Connectors** if you plan to send the staged emails directly from chat.

## Step-by-step guide

\<Steps\>

  \<Step title="Select or Invoke the Workflow"\>

    Open DeepVista Chat and invoke the workflow directly by referencing it or selecting it from your installed skills:

    \`\`\`text

    Run Workflow Lead Research & Enrichment

    \`\`\`

</Frame>

    \<Tip\>

      You can also trigger this workflow automatically on a cron cadence or via inbound webhooks by attaching a `schedule_job`.

    \</Tip\>

  \</Step\>

  \<Step title="Provide Target Segment Inputs"\>

    DeepVista prompts you for required inputs before running:

    - **Target Segment** `target_segment`): Define the ICP segment or paste specific target company names (e.g., `"DeepVista.ai"` or `"Top 10 DeFi lending protocols on Ethereum & Arbitrum"`).

    - **Existing Leads** `existing_leads`): Optionally pull candidate organization cards directly from VistaBase.

    - **Notification Email** `outreach_contact_email`): Optional email address to receive a run summary upon completion.

    Select **Provide input now** and specify your parameters.

    \[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER 2\]

  \</Step\>

  \<Step title="Automated Research & Lead Scoring"\>

    DeepVista executes the multi-phase pipeline in sequence:

    1. **Define ICP & Source Candidates**: Compiles candidate targets from web search `exa_search`) and VistaBase.

    2. **Research Each Lead**: Scrapes company background, funding stage, key decision-makers, and public risk/urgency signals.

    3. **Score & Prioritize**: Ranks each lead on a 0–10 scale across ICP fit, urgency, reachability, and budget.

    You can monitor live progress on the interactive Mermaid workflow graph as nodes advance from `:::dvTodo` to `:::dvActive` and `:::dvDone`.

    \[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER 3\]

  \</Step\>

  \<Step title="Review Enriched Context Cards in VistaBase"\>

    Once research completes, DeepVista creates or updates structured context cards:

    - **Organization Card**: Contains product overview, funding details, decision-makers, and ICP score rationale.

    - **Person Card**: Profile for identified decision-makers (e.g., Founder/CEO, Risk Lead).

    - **Graph Links**: Automatically connects person cards to organization cards and the active workflow run.

    \<Frame\>

      \<contextCardBlock id="631de0e7-fc99-4f39-9755-85739caf7265" cardType="organization" view="compact"\>

        [DeepVista.ai](http://DeepVista.ai)

        Lead research and ICP profile updated with product focus, key contacts, and fit score.

      \</contextCardBlock\>

    \</Frame\>

    \[SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER 4\]

  \</Step\>

  \<Step title="Review & Send Staged Outreach Email"\>

    For top-priority leads (Score 8–10), DeepVista drafts a short, specific outreach note referencing exact findings from research.

    The draft is saved as an `email` card with formatted `to:` and `subject:` frontmatter headers. It remains unsent until you review and trigger delivery.

    \<Note\>

      To deliver the email, click the staged email card and hit **Send**, or instruct chat: `"Send the outreach draft to Jing Wang"`.
