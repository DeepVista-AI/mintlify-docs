## Ask DeepVista to Draft Outreach Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask DeepVista to draft outreach emails">
    Ask DeepVista:

    ```text
    Help me draft outreach emails for all of the leads.
    ```

    DeepVista will save your email drafts as context cards.

    <Frame>
      ![Drafted outreach email with a Send button, personalized to the lead and their company](/images/image-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and edit the draft">
    Click into the email card to view and edit the draft.

    To make changes, you can also ask DeepVista directly:

    ```text
    Add my email signature.
    ```

    DeepVista will automatically pull the necessary information from your VistaBase context cards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect Your Gmail and Send Your Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Gmail">
    To send out an email, click **Send** and connect your Gmail.

    <Frame>
      ![Settings page with the User menu open, showing the Gmail data connector](/images/image-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant access">
    Make sure DeepVista has all the access it needs.

    <Frame>
      ![Google OAuth consent screen showing the Gmail permissions DeepVista requests](/images/image-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Upon success, you'll see the confirmation page below.

    <Frame>
      ![Confirmation screen after Gmail is successfully connected](/images/image-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the email">
    Once ready, click **Send** in the bottom-right corner, or ask DeepVista to send all of them automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm delivery">
    To confirm, open Gmail and find the sent email in your Gmail "Sent" tab.

    <Frame>
      ![The sent outreach email as received in the lead's inbox](/images/image-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask DeepVista to Schedule a Daily Job

<Steps>
  <Step title="Schedule the daily motion">
    Ask DeepVista:

    ```text
    Schedule a daily task to source 20 new leads every day. Never repeat
    companies already in the tracker.
    ```

    DeepVista will create an automated **workflow** and a **scheduled job** for you.

    <Frame>
      ![Scheduled job confirmation, running on a daily cadence](/images/icp-scheduled-job.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and adjust">
    Click the context cards to view details and make changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
