## Overview

Finding high-fit leads and crafting hyper-personalized outreach usually requires juggling multiple tabs, manual web searches, and fragmented notes.

With the **Lead Research & Enrichment** workflow, DeepVista automates this end-to-end pipeline:

1. Sources candidates based on your target ICP segment.
2. Researches company fundamentals, funding stage, and key decision-makers.
3. Scores and prioritizes leads against your exact criteria.
4. Saves enriched organization and person cards into VistaBase™, automatically linking them in the knowledge graph.
5. Stages a tailored outreach draft (an `email` card) for your final review before sending.

This tutorial walks through invoking the workflow, providing inputs, and reviewing what it generates.

## Why DeepVista for lead research?

- **Persistent context**: unlike standalone scraping tools, DeepVista checks your existing VistaBase™ cards first to avoid duplicate contacts and build on notes from prior sessions.
- **Staged and safe execution**: outreach drafts are saved as unsent `email` cards. Nothing is sent externally without your review and approval.
- **Connected knowledge base**: every enriched lead, key contact, and outreach draft is cross-linked in your knowledge graph, so your pipeline stays traceable end to end.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

1. A DeepVista account with an active project
2. The **Lead Research & Enrichment** workflow installed in your workspace (or available in the Skill catalog)
3. (Optional) [Gmail connected](/tutorials/gmail-daily-summary-workflow) if you plan to send the staged emails directly from chat

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select or invoke the workflow">
    Open DeepVista chat and invoke the workflow directly by name:

    ```text
    Run Workflow: Lead Research & Enrichment
    ```

    <Tip>
      You can also trigger this workflow automatically on a schedule or via inbound webhooks by attaching a `schedule_job`.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provide target segment inputs">
    DeepVista prompts you for the inputs it needs before running:

    - **Target segment or lead list** (`target_segment`, required): the ICP segment or list of candidate companies to research.
    - **Existing leads** (`existing_leads`, optional): pull candidate organization cards directly from VistaBase™.
    - **Notification email** (`outreach_contact_email`, optional): where to send a run summary once it completes.

    Select **Provide input now** and enter your parameters.

    <Frame>
      ![DeepVista prompting for target segment, existing leads, and a notification email before running the workflow](/images/image-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Automated research and lead scoring">
    DeepVista runs the pipeline in sequence:

    1. **Source candidates**: compiles candidate targets from web search and VistaBase™.
    2. **Research each lead**: looks up company background, funding stage, and key decision-makers.
    3. **Score and prioritize**: ranks each lead on a 0 to 10 scale across ICP fit, urgency, reachability, and budget.

    You can monitor progress on the workflow's live run view as each step advances from queued to running to done.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review enriched context cards in VistaBase">
    Once research completes, DeepVista creates or updates structured context cards:

    - **Organization card**: product overview, funding details, decision-makers, and ICP score rationale.
    - **Person card**: profile for each identified decision-maker.
    - **Graph links**: person cards are automatically connected to their organization card and to the workflow run that created them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and send the staged outreach email">
    For top-priority leads, DeepVista drafts a short, specific outreach note referencing exact findings from research.

    The draft is saved as an `email` card with `to` and `subject` fields filled in. It stays unsent until you review and approve it.

    <Note>
      To send it, open the staged email card and click **Send**, or tell chat: `"Send the outreach draft to [contact name]"`.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
