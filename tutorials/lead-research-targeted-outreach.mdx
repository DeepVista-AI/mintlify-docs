## Ask DeepVista to Draft Outreach Email

1. Ask DeepVista: "Help me draft outreach emails for all of the leads." DeepVista will save your email drafts as context cards.

   <Frame>
     ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-outreach-drafts-list.png)
   </Frame>

2. Click into the email card to view and edit the draft.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-outreach-email-draft.png)
</Frame>

To make changes, you can also ask DeepVista directly: "Add my email signature."

DeepVista will automatically pull the necessary information from your VistaBase context cards.

## Connect Your Gmail and Send Your Email

3. To send out an email, Click **Send** and connect your Gmail.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-connect-gmail-modal.png)
</Frame>

4. Make sure DeepVista has all the access it needs.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-gmail-oauth-consent.png)
</Frame>

5. Upon success, you'll see the confirmation page below.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-gmail-connected.png)
</Frame>

7\. Once ready, click **Send** in the bottom-right corner, or ask DeepVista to send all of them automatically.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-outreach-review-send.png)
</Frame>

8\. To confirm, open Gmail and find the sent email in your Gmail "Sent" tab.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-gmail-sent-tab.png)
</Frame>

## Ask DeepVista to Schedule a Daily Job

1. Ask DeepVista: *"Schedule a daily task to source 20 new leads every day. Never repeat companies already in the tracker."* DeepVista will create an automated **workflow** and a **scheduled job** for you.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-daily-job-cards.png)
</Frame>

2. Click the context cards to view details and make changes.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-daily-job-detail.gif)
</Frame>
