With your tracker set up, this step builds your first outbound workflow: connect Gmail once, then let DeepVista find and reach out to ICP-fit accounts automatically every day.

## Connect Gmail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Gmail data connector">
    Click your account menu, then select **+ Gmail**.

    <Frame>
      ![Settings page with the User menu open, showing the Gmail data connector](/images/image-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant access">
    Make sure DeepVista has the following access.

    <Frame>
      ![Google OAuth consent screen showing the Gmail permissions DeepVista requests](/images/image-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Upon success, you'll see this page and get redirected to the home page automatically.

    <Frame>
      ![Confirmation screen after Gmail is successfully connected](/images/image-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create Your First Outbound Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your company and what you want automated">
    In DeepVista chat, give your company website and describe the daily outbound motion you want. For example:

    ```text
    Here's my company website: https://www.yourcompany.com. Help me
    find 20 ICPs and send tailored outreach email every day. Schedule it
    as a daily workflow.
    ```

    <Frame>
      ![DeepVista chat building a Daily ICP Lead Sourcing & Tailored Outreach workflow from a company website prompt](/images/icp-workflow-prompt.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the workflow DeepVista builds">
    DeepVista lays out the workflow as a clear sequence, with an approval gate before anything sends:

    <Frame>
      ![Workflow diagram: Input, Source and Qualify 20 ICP Leads, Draft Tailored Outreach Emails, Approve: Send Emails, Review and Deliver Outreach, Output](/images/icp-workflow-diagram.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the daily schedule">
    The workflow runs on its own from here on, at the cadence you asked for.

    <Frame>
      ![Scheduled job confirmation for the Daily ICP Sourcing and Tailored Outreach Job, running weekdays at 9:00 AM](/images/icp-scheduled-job.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="See an example of what it drafts">
    Each run stages a personalized outreach email referencing real findings about the target account, ready for you to review before it sends.

    <Frame>
      ![Example staged outreach email, reviewed and edited before sending](/images/icp-outreach-example.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
