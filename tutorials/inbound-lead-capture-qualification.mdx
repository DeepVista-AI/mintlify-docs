## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

1. A DeepVista account with an active project
2. A Discord webhook URL configured for your team's channel
3. [Gmail connected](/tutorials/lead-research-targeted-outreach) to DeepVista, so drafted replies can be sent on your behalf
4. An intake form (or another way for inquiries to reach DeepVista, like a forwarded email)

## Set Up Public Web Input Forms

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the input form">
    Navigate to your active workflow and click **Settings**. Locate the **Input Form** section and toggle it on. This publishes a public form generated directly from your workflow's input configuration.

    <Frame>
      ![Enable Input Form](/images/ex3-1.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify form labels">
    Review the public form's appearance to ensure all field labels and descriptions accurately reflect the information you want to collect.

    <Frame>
      ![Verify Form Labels](/images/ex3-4.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and share the link">
    Copy the generated public form link, open it in a new window, and submit a test response to verify it kicks off your automated run.

    <Frame>
      ![Test Form Submission](/images/ex3-2.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auto-Score ICP Fit & Populate Tracker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the form submission">
    DeepVista extracts the company domain from the work email and checks the knowledge base first, so the same lead never gets recorded twice. It then creates or updates a **person** card, an **organization** card, and a **message** card, all linked together in your graph.

    <Frame>
      ![Person card and message card generated from a form submission, showing contact details and the inquiry text](/images/image-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate identity and score ICP fit">
    DeepVista researches the lead across public sources to verify their actual identity, cross-checking role and organization to rule out name collisions. It then evaluates the lead against your ICP parameters: AI-native fit, stage and team size, and operational need. Findings are saved as an ICP Analysis card linked to both the lead and organization cards.

    <Frame>
      ![ICP Analysis card showing a 9.5 out of 10 fit score with a criteria breakdown](/images/image-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Populate your tracker">
    The new person and organization cards attach as a row in the customer tracker you set up in Step 1, so every qualified lead shows up there without manual entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Route Real-Time Discord Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate a Discord webhook">
    Go to **Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks**. Click **New Webhook**, name it, select your channel, and copy the webhook URL.

    <Frame>
      ![Creating a Discord Webhook](/images/web11.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable live channel notifications">
    Paste the copied URL into DeepVista's outbound notification node. Alerts post **green** (`#10B981`) for high-priority fit (score 9.0 or higher), and **amber** (`#F59E0B`) for standard fit or an ambiguous profile that needs manual review.

    <Frame>
      ![Finalizing DeepVista to Discord link](/images/web4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send an Instant Reply with a Booking Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review and send the staged reply">
    For high-fit leads, DeepVista drafts a concise email reply, including a direct pilot proposal and a calendar booking link. Review the staged draft inside DeepVista chat and approve delivery with a single instruction or click. DeepVista then sends the email through your connected Gmail account.

    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![The sent outreach email as received in the lead's inbox](/images/image-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
