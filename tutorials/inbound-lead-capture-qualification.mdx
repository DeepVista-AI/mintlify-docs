## Capture Inbound Leads

1. Ask DeepVista: Help me create an early-access signup workflow.

2. Click into the context card to **Run** the workflow.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-signup-workflow-diagram.gif)
</Frame>

3. You'll be asked to set up an input form. Hover over to the upper right corner and generate a URL for your input form.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-signup-workflow-detail-1.gif)
</Frame>

4. To customize the input form, on the workflow page, click **"Input" \>"Input Form" \> "Add input**" to get a sample prompt you can modify easily.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-signup-workflow-detail-2.gif)
</Frame>

## Get Notified with Inbound Activities

5. You can now share the input form URL with potential leads via email.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-signup-form-email.png)
</Frame>

6. Once someone fills out the form, you'll get an email notification.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-form-submission-notification.png)
</Frame>

## Follow up with High-fit Leads

7. Capture a screenshot of the message. Copy and paste to DeepVista. Ask it: Create a follow up email for me.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-followup-request-chat.png)
</Frame>

8. DeepVista will search across VistaBase to retrieve relevant context about this person, email, and relationship.
9. In the email card, edit and hit **Send**.

   <Frame>
     ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-followup-email-draft.png)
   </Frame>
