## Overview

**Speed to lead** is one of the highest-leverage conversion metrics for growth teams: responding to an inbound inquiry within minutes significantly increases meeting booking and pipeline conversion rates. However, for lean teams, manual SDR triage—verifying sender identities, researching company background, scoring ICP fit, and drafting tailored replies—frequently creates hours of delay, allowing high-intent leads to drop off.

By running the **Inbound Lead Capture & Qualification** workflow in DeepVista, you automate the triage loop instantly upon form submission to drive sub-minute speed to lead:

1. **Maximizes speed to lead**: automatically researches the lead, calculates ICP fit, and stages a personalized email response with booking links so reps can approve and send in a single click.
2. **Eliminates manual data entry**: structures raw submission fields into connected `person`, `organization`, and `message` context cards within your knowledge graph.
3. **Cross-validates identity and ICP fit**: verifies public professional records, resolves title ambiguities, and scores fit against your ideal customer criteria.
4. **Notifies your team in real time**: posts color-coded priority alerts directly to your team's Discord channel for instant visibility.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

1. A DeepVista account with an active project
2. A Discord webhook URL configured for your team's channel
3. [Gmail connected](/tutorials/gmail-daily-summary-workflow) to DeepVista, so drafted replies can be sent on your behalf
4. An intake form (or another way for inquiries to reach DeepVista, like a forwarded email)

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the form submission">
    Upon receiving a form submission (first name, last name, work email, phone number, message), DeepVista extracts the company domain from the work email and checks the knowledge base first, so the same lead never gets recorded twice.

    DeepVista then creates or updates:

    - A **person** card for the lead
    - An **organization** card for their company
    - A **message** card recording their specific inquiry, word for word

    All three are linked together in your graph, so past interactions surface automatically in future sessions.

    <Frame>
      ![Person card and message card generated from a form submission, showing contact details and the inquiry text](/images/image-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate identity and score ICP fit">
    DeepVista researches the lead across public sources to verify their actual identity, cross-checking role and organization to rule out name collisions.

    It then evaluates the lead against your ICP parameters, for example:

    - **AI native**: building AI-first products, or deeply integrating AI workflows?
    - **Stage and team size**: does their funding stage and team size match your target range?
    - **Operational need**: does their situation show demand for leverage over headcount?

    Findings are saved as an ICP Analysis card (e.g. _9.5 / 10, strong fit_) linked to both the lead and organization cards.

    <Frame>
      ![ICP Analysis card showing a 9.5 out of 10 fit score with a criteria breakdown](/images/image-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get notified in Discord">
    DeepVista formats the lead details, company info, and ICP score into a Discord alert:

    - **Green** (`#10B981`): high-priority ICP fit (score 9.0 or higher)
    - **Amber** (`#F59E0B`): standard fit, or an ambiguous profile that needs manual review

    The alert posts directly to your team's configured Discord channel via webhook, so your team has immediate visibility without opening DeepVista.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send the staged outreach email">
    For high-fit leads, DeepVista drafts a concise email reply, including a direct pilot proposal and a calendar booking link.

    You can review the staged draft inside DeepVista chat and approve delivery with a single instruction or click. DeepVista then sends the email through your connected Gmail account.

    <Frame>
      ![Drafted outreach email with a Send button, personalized to the lead and their company](/images/image-7.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![The sent outreach email as received in the lead's inbox](/images/image-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
