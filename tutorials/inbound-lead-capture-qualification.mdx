## Capture Inbound Leads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the signup workflow">
    Ask DeepVista:

    ```text
    Help me create an early-access signup workflow.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the workflow">
    Click into the context card to **Run** the workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the input form URL">
    You'll be asked to set up an input form. Hover over to the upper right corner and generate a URL for your input form.

    <Frame>
      ![Generating a public URL for the input form](/images/ex3-1.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the input form">
    To customize the input form, on the workflow page, click **Input** → **Input Form** → **Add input** to get a sample prompt you can modify easily.

    <Frame>
      ![Customizing input form field labels](/images/ex3-4.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Get Notified with Inbound Activities

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share the form">
    You can now share the input form URL with potential leads via email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get notified">
    Once someone fills out the form, you'll get an email notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow up with High-fit Leads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for a follow-up">
    Capture a screenshot of the message. Copy and paste it to DeepVista and ask:

    ```text
    Create a follow up email for me.
    ```

    DeepVista will search across VistaBase to retrieve relevant context about this person, email, and relationship.

    <Frame>
      ![A drafted follow-up email with options to send via Gmail or refine the draft](/images/tracker-followup-draft.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    In the email card, edit and hit **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
