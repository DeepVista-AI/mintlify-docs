## 1. Research Leads and Set Up a CRM Tracker

### Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have a DeepVista account with an active project.

To create an account, [sign up here](https://app.deepvista.ai/login?redirectUrl=%2Fsign%2520in).

Once you're in, you'll land on a blank homepage, ready to start.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-homepage.png)
</Frame>

### Onboard Your Company and Ask DeepVista to Search Leads for You

<Steps>
  <Step title='Ask DeepVista: "Here&apos;s my company website: https://www.yourcompany.com. Help me find 20 leads."'>
    DeepVista will learn your value propositions, ICPs, and key capabilities, then research and generate a **table card**, which serves as your CRM tracker 1.0.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-crm-tracker-created.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the table card to expand the full list.">
    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-crm-tracker-expanded.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title='To view details for a specific entry, hover over the table row and click "Open."'>
    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-crm-tracker-open-entry.gif)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Customize Your Search Fields

1. To retrieve additional information, prompt DeepVista: "Please add a column for the LinkedIn page. Research and populate it." The system will automatically add and populate the new column with the requested data.

## 2. Draft, Send, and Schedule Outreach Email

### Ask DeepVista to Draft Outreach Email

<Steps>
  <Step title='Ask DeepVista: "Help me draft outreach emails for all of the leads."'>
    DeepVista will save your email drafts as context cards.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-outreach-drafts-list.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click into the email card to view and edit the draft.">
    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-outreach-email-draft.png)
    </Frame>

    To make changes, you can also ask DeepVista directly: "Add my email signature."

    DeepVista will automatically pull the necessary information from your VistaBase context cards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Connect Your Gmail and Send Your Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="To send out an email, Click Send and connect your Gmail.">
    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-connect-gmail-modal.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make sure DeepVista has all the access it needs.">
    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-gmail-oauth-consent.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upon success, you'll see the confirmation page below.">
    <Frame>
      ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-gmail-connected.png)

  <Step title="Once ready, click Send in the bottom-right corner, or ask DeepVista to send all of them automatically.">
    <Frame>
