## Overview

By the end, you'll have a public form link that captures lead details, starts your workflow, and emails you each submission.

DeepVista can generate a public input form from the inputs already configured on your workflow — fields like name, email, or company — so you don't build a separate form app.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

1. A DeepVista account with an active project
2. A workflow with the input fields you want to collect (for example Name, Email, Company)

<Note>
  You can edit these input fields any time if your data needs change. Configure them before you share the public link.
</Note>

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the input form">
    Open your active workflow, then click **Settings**. In the **Input Form** section, toggle it **On**. DeepVista publishes a public form generated from your workflow's input configuration.

    ![Enable Input Form](/images/ex3-1.gif)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify form labels">
    Review the public form so every field label and description matches what you want people to enter.

    ![Verify Form Labels](/images/ex3-4.gif)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test and share the link">
    Copy the generated public form link and open it in a new window. Submit a test response to confirm the form starts a workflow run.

    ![Test Form Submission](/images/ex3-2.gif)
  </Step>
</Steps>

***

<Tip>
  **What happens next?**
  Every time someone fills out your public link, DeepVista **triggers a new workflow run** and **emails you the submission details** so you don't miss a lead.
</Tip>
