Transform raw website contact submissions into structured graph context, verified ICP ratings, instant Discord alerts, and personalized outreach drafts—all automatically.

## Overview

When prospective customers submit a contact form, rapid qualification and response are critical. Manual lead processing leads to delayed follow-ups, incomplete CRM records, and missed high-value accounts.

By running the **Inbound Lead Capture** workflow in DeepVista, you convert raw form submissions into an automated pipeline that:

1. **Eliminates Manual Data Entry:** Automatically structures lead details into connected `person`, `organization`, and `message` Context Cards in your knowledge graph.
2. **Cross-Validates Identity & ICP Fit:** Researches lead background across web sources, resolves identity ambiguities, and rates account fit against your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
3. **Notifies the Team Real-Time:** Routes color-coded rich-embed alerts directly into your team's Discord channel based on lead priority.
4. **Stages High-Agency Outreach:** Generates concise, ready-to-send email reply drafts with calendar booking links for fast founder approval.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Upon receiving form submission fields (\`first\_name\`, \`last\_name\`, \`work\_email\`, \`phone\_number\`, \`message\`), DeepVista extracts the company domain and queries the knowledge base to prevent duplicate records.

```text
DeepVista creates or updates:
- A `person`card for the lead (e.g., Shayla Liao).
- An `organization`card for their company (FounderCoHo).
- A `message`card recording their specific inquiry text.

All cards are linked bi-directionally in your graph database so past interactions surface automatically in future sessions.
```

The payload posts directly to your team's designated Discord channel via webhook, giving immediate visibility to team members.
