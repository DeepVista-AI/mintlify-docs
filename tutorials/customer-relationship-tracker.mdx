## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have a DeepVista account with an active project.

To create an account, [sign up here](https://app.deepvista.ai/login?redirectUrl=%2Fsign%2520in).

Once you're in, you'll land on a blank homepage, ready to start.

## Onboard Your Company

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell DeepVista about your company">
    In chat, provide your company website:

    ```text
    https://www.yourcompany.com
    ```

    DeepVista will learn your value propositions, ICPs, and key capabilities. This data is saved as an **organization card** within VistaBase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ask DeepVista to Search Leads for You

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for new leads">
    Ask DeepVista:

    ```text
    Help me find 20 new leads.
    ```

    DeepVista will research and generate a **table card**, which serves as your CRM tracker.

    <Frame>
      ![DeepVista confirming the Customer CRM Tracker table card was created](/images/image-13.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand the full list">
    Click the table card to expand the full list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View details for an entry">
    To view details for a specific entry, hover over the table row and click **Open**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customize Your Search Fields

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a custom column">
    To retrieve additional information, prompt DeepVista:

    ```text
    Please add a column for the LinkedIn page. Research and populate it.
    ```

    DeepVista will automatically add and populate the new column with the requested data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
