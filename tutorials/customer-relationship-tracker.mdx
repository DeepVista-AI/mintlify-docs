## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have a DeepVista account with an active project.

To create an account, [sign up here](https://app.deepvista.ai/login?redirectUrl=%2Fsign%2520in).

Once you're in, you'll land on a blank homepage, ready to start.

  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-homepage.png)
</Frame>

## Onboard Your Company and Ask DeepVista to Search Leads for You

<Steps>
  <Step title='Ask DeepVista: "Here&apos;s my company website: https://www.yourcompany.com. Help me find 20 leads."'>
    DeepVista will learn your value propositions, ICPs, and key capabilities, then research and generate a **table card**, which serves as your CRM tracker 1.0.
