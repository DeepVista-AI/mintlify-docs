## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have a DeepVista account with an active project.

To create an account, [sign up here](https://app.deepvista.ai/login?redirectUrl=%2Fsign%2520in).

Once you're in, you'll land on a blank homepage, ready to start.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-homepage.png)
</Frame>

## Onboard Your Company

1. In chat, provide your company website: [https://www.yourcompany.com](https://www.yourcompany.com). DeepVista will learn your value propositions, ICPs, and key capabilities.

   This data is saved as an **organization card** within VistaBase.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-org-card.png)
</Frame>

## Ask DeepVista to Search Leads for You

2. Ask DeepVista: "Help me find 20 new leads."

   DeepVista will research and generate a **table card**, which serves as your CRM tracker.

   <Frame>
     ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-crm-tracker-created.png)
   </Frame>

3. Click the table card to expand the full list.

   <Frame>
     ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-crm-tracker-expanded.png)
   </Frame>

4. To view details for a specific entry, hover over the table row and click "**Open**."

   <Frame>
     ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-crm-tracker-open-entry.gif)
   </Frame>

## Customize Your Search Fields

5. To retrieve additional information, prompt DeepVista: "Please add a column for the LinkedIn page. Research and populate it."

   The system will automatically add and populate the new column with the requested data.

   <Frame>
     ![](/images/lightworkflow-doc-crm-tracker-linkedin-column.png)
   </Frame>
